# Scene System

## Scene Selection

Choose a scene because its space, practical lights, circulation, and social behavior support the user's action and emotion. Treat each entry as a grammar, not a fixed template.

## Period And Location Anchors

Use two to four grounded anchors, not all at once:

- red urban taxi with silver roof, period-appropriate compact dashboard and analog meter cues;
- primrose-yellow public light bus with red or green roof when relevant to the route and post-1995 period;
- double-decker bus, tram, metal rail, tiled entrance, painted road marking, or compact bus stop;
- vertical or projecting shop signs integrated with building facades rather than floating holograms;
- stainless counters, red plastic cups, patterned laminate, mosaic or ceramic tile, metal shutters, window bars, fluorescent tubes, utility pipes, peeling paint, worn wood, and plastic stools;
- everyday late-twentieth-century clothing and bags rather than costume-pageant styling.

Do not force a famous landmark, exact street name, brand, or readable sign. The scene should remain plausible when no landmark is visible.

## Scene-to-Emotion Defaults

- **Waiting:** street corner, crossing edge, convenience-store entrance, bus interior, apartment corridor.
- **Searching or rushing:** crossing, alley, subway entrance, footbridge, stairwell.
- **Restrained intimacy:** night restaurant, old café, apartment corridor, stairwell, taxi rear seat.
- **Memory:** taxi window, rainy window, balcony, hotel corridor, repeated street corner.
- **Restless solitude:** bus, taxi, convenience store, narrow alley, late restaurant.
- **Humid languor:** humid rented room, tiled washroom, old café, apartment corridor.
- **Provisional intimacy or exhaustion:** temporary domestic room, late restaurant, taxi, rooftop edge.

## Scene Integrity Check

Before compiling, verify:

- the subject can physically perform the requested action in the space;
- the chosen practical lights could exist in that space;
- the motion recipe has a plausible moving plane;
- the period cues do not contradict one another;
- the scene reads as lived-in Hong Kong without relying on a skyline, flag, or the word “Hong Kong.”
