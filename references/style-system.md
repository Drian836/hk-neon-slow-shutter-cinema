# Hong Kong Urban Cinema Style System

## Identity

Chinese: 九十年代香港都市霓虹慢快门电影影像

English: Lived-in late-twentieth-century Hong Kong urban slow-shutter cinema

Create a human-scale city image where time, movement, practical light, compressed space, and emotional distance are visible. The city must feel inhabited and specific rather than futuristic, decorative, or assembled from neon keywords.

## Fixed System

- **Urban domain:** dense, ordinary Hong Kong spaces from the late 1980s to around 2000: working streets, small businesses, transport, public circulation, and worn residential interiors.
- **Human in environment:** keep the person physically embedded in the location. Surroundings, foreground objects, reflections, passersby, and architecture participate in the story.
- **Relative time behavior:** make time perceptible as a relationship between at least two clocks: subject versus crowd, camera-plus-subject versus architecture, vehicle interior versus passing city, or held gesture versus repeated route. Directional motion, discontinuous edge residue, repetition, and suspended gesture are tools; blur alone is not the identity.
- **Practical light:** motivate color from fluorescent tubes, tungsten bulbs, street lamps, signs, vehicle lights, windows, counters, or reflected spill.
- **Layered space:** establish foreground, subject plane, and background whenever the scene permits. Use occlusion or reflection purposefully.
- **Imperfect reproduction:** retain color-film grain, soft highlight bloom, slight halation, mixed white balance, color contamination, imperfect exposure, and deep shadows with selective information.
- **Emotional field:** favor longing, solitude, waiting, missed contact, restrained intimacy, memory, restlessness, searching, or fleeting relief.
- **Tactile camera presence:** declare where the camera is relative to the subject and world. Allow close wide-angle perspective, tracking companionship, locked threshold observation, handheld drift, edge cropping, and imperfect framing when they serve the relationship.
- **Family separation:** route through one primary family in `visual-families.md`; do not collapse humid interior stillness, two-clock street flux, wide-angle night estrangement, restrained corridor repetition, and cramped relational instability into one palette.
- **Color subordination:** choose palette only after time, camera, blocking, and practical-light geometry. Color grading can reinforce a design but never create the family by itself.

## Variable System

- Frame: 3:2 or 16:9 landscape by default; 1:1, 4:5, or vertical only when the request or source benefits from it.
- Time: blue hour, evening rush, late night, after midnight, pre-dawn, rainy day, or fluorescent interior daytime.
- Scene: select from `scene-system.md` according to action and emotion.
- Motion: strong temporal contrast, moderate directional smear, subtle echo, camera drift, or still tension.
- Composition: close wide-angle, occluded medium shot, deep environmental portrait, reflected fragment, or locked corridor view.
- Palette: one dominant practical family, one secondary temperature, and no more than one small accent.
- Texture: coarse high-speed color grain, softer fine grain, pushed underexposure, halation, slight gate weave, or muted print softness.
- Emotional temperature: restless, tender, guarded, weary, anxious, playful, numb, or quietly hopeful.

## Emotional System

Map emotion to visible behavior:

| Emotion | Subject behavior | Time behavior | Spatial behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting | still posture, gaze beyond frame | faster crowd or suspended surroundings | subject isolated inside circulation |
| Searching | walking, turning, looking past camera | uneven directional movement | obstructed sight lines and deep routes |
| Missed encounter | two paths that nearly meet | opposing movement directions | separation by door, passerby, glass, or edge |
| Restless solitude | contained face or repetitive gesture | frame echoes or camera drift | close body with receding city depth |
| Restrained intimacy | minimal gesture, shared silence | little or no blur | doorway, corridor, table, or adjacent planes |
| Memory | still subject, reflection, or absent space | subtle temporal echo | layered glass, mirror, window, or repeated view |
| Urgency | run, hurry, board, cross | strong directional smear | diagonal route, vehicle passage, or crowd pressure |

Do not represent emotion only through facial expression. Use distance, blocking, movement, light, and environment.

## Spatial System

- Prefer compact, plausible rooms and streets over monumental skylines.
- Preserve signs of daily use: stacked cups, tiled walls, hanging laundry, metal shutters, worn paint, plastic stools, handrails, utility pipes, window bars, condensation, receipts, and traffic markings.
- Select only details appropriate to the chosen scene; do not turn period cues into a prop inventory.
- Use transport accurately when visible: urban taxis may use red lower bodywork with a silver roof; period public light buses may use a primrose-yellow body with red or green roof; double-decker buses and trams are valid when geographically plausible.
- Keep readable brand names and exact signage optional and generic. Avoid invented gibberish as a focal point.

## Film Reproduction System

- Treat grain as an image-wide material structure, not a noise overlay concentrated only in shadows.
- Let bright practicals bloom or halo slightly; do not turn every edge into glow.
- Allow mixed color temperatures and modest skin-tone contamination from the actual environment.
- Preserve some shadow separation around faces, hands, and key objects even when the overall exposure is dark.
- Permit slight softness, focus falloff, gate weave, or frame-edge imperfection; keep the emotional focal information readable.
- Do not lock the system to one restoration, transfer, LUT, stock number, or aspect ratio. Relative color and light behavior are more stable than any single release grade.

## Anti-Identity

Hong Kong night is not generic cyberpunk. Avoid:

- futuristic city, sci-fi technology, holograms, flying vehicles, or Blade-Runner-like worldbuilding;
- clean purple-blue RGB neon, gaming aesthetics, rainbow neon on every surface, or synthetic volumetric fog;
- wet asphalt as a mandatory cliché when rain is not motivated;
- sterile modern malls, spotless luxury interiors, contemporary LED architecture, or glass-tower establishing shots as the default;
- luxury commercial portraits, beauty retouching, fashion-campaign posing, studio key/fill/rim lighting, or glossy advertising polish;
- American noir imitation, fedora-and-venetian-blind shorthand, or Hollywood blockbuster lighting;
- clean orange-teal grading, perfect HDR, perfect digital sharpness, or evenly lifted shadows;
- anime, kawaii, comic, vaporwave, synthwave, or Y2K gaming graphics;
- perfectly centered commercial composition, isolated 85 mm beauty portrait, empty blurred background, or pristine bokeh separation;
- uniform full-frame motion blur, radial software blur, duplicated faces, or light trails unrelated to a moving source;
- copying a recognizable actor, exact film still, exact costume, subtitle, brand, location, or shot design from a reference.
- transformations in which the source frame remains structurally identical after only hue, contrast, exposure, wetness, grain, glow, or generic blur are removed.
