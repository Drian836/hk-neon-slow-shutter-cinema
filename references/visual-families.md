# Visual Families And Routing

Use film titles only to understand the user's intent. Compile every result from visible behavior and never copy an actor, costume, prop, location, title treatment, or exact shot.

## Shared Core

Every family must retain the same core:

- a person physically embedded in a working space;
- emotion expressed through blocking, attention, circulation, and distance;
- time made visible through relative clocks, repetition, suspended gesture, or selective motion;
- a camera with a position inside the scene rather than a neutral beauty-portrait viewpoint;
- practical light with a visible source and localized color contamination;
- imperfect film reproduction that supports, rather than substitutes for, scene design.

Color is not a family selector by itself.

## Family A — Humid Interior Drift

Intent signals: early-1990s Hong Kong melancholy, tropical languor, `Days of Being Wild` / `阿飞正传`, solitary room, mirror, clock, waiting, sleepless heat.

Visible grammar:

- compact rooms, tiled washrooms, old cafés, narrow hallways, louvered windows, ceiling fans, worn paint, humid glass, and daylight or practical spill filtered through surfaces;
- medium-wide or normal observation, off-axis bodies, mirror fragments, doorframes, and objects that divide the frame;
- mostly stable time with one repetitive cue such as fan movement, smoke, curtain drift, clock presence, or a slow turn;
- greenish daylight, dirty cream, tobacco amber, muted red-brown, and deep but separated shadows;
- languid posture, indirect gaze, and emotional vacancy rather than overt performance.

Do not reduce this family to green-orange skin. If the room, obstruction, stillness, and humid material response are absent, the family is absent.

## Family B — Urban Temporal Flux

Intent signals: `Chungking Express` / `重庆森林`, street pursuit, crossing crowd, fast food counter, missed connection, time running out, step printing, 抽帧, 快慢错位.

Visible grammar:

- one readable anchor moving at a different apparent clock from passersby, traffic, signs, or the camera;
- discontinuous motion residues: uneven edge echoes, broken pedestrian smears, segmented source-linked light traces, and a slightly stuttering spatial cadence;
- handheld or tracking proximity in working streets, crossings, counters, concourses, escalators, and cramped shops;
- chance occlusions and imperfect headroom that make the camera feel caught in circulation;
- mixed fluorescent, warm counter light, dirty street light, and small localized sign accents.

Default time contract: stable or partly stable face/torso + faster surrounding clock + visible uneven cadence. Smooth background blur alone is insufficient.

## Family C — Nocturnal Wide-Angle Estrangement

Intent signals: `Fallen Angels` / `堕落天使`, night ride, tunnel, stairwell, extreme proximity, alienation, low or high corner, distorted city depth.

Visible grammar:

- camera extremely close to a subject while a large amount of environment remains legible;
- purposeful wide-angle near/far exaggeration, edge stretching, off-axis face placement, unusual high/low camera position, and foreground body or architecture entering the lens space;
- tunnels, tiled stairwells, late-night shops, back rooms, vehicles, service corridors, and fluorescent passages;
- camera and subject may move together while walls, tiles, railings, or lights sweep past at a second clock;
- sour fluorescent green, sodium or tungsten amber, occasional deep red, clipped practicals, and dense underexposed edges.

Protect identity-critical facial geometry. Distort space more than eyes, nose, mouth, or jaw. Fisheye caricature is a failure.

## Family D — Restrained Corridor Memory

Intent signals: `In the Mood for Love` / `花样年华`, withheld intimacy, corridor, doorway, adjacent rooms, repetition, two people sharing silence.

Visible grammar:

- repeated architecture, narrow routes, doorframes, curtains, wall edges, mirrors, or furniture that partially withhold the subjects;
- two people staged on adjacent or unequal depth planes rather than in a conventional romantic two-shot;
- restrained movement, repeated passage, held glances, small hand actions, or a secondary passerby; overt slow shutter is optional and often inappropriate;
- warm practical pools against dark red, green, tobacco, or neutral shadow; light arrives around corners and through thresholds;
- locked or gently observing camera, compressed routes, deliberate negative obstruction, and recurring spatial rhythm.

This film is historically set before the 1990s. For a requested 1990s Hong Kong output, transfer its obstruction, repetition, distance, and light logic without importing period-specific costume or décor.

## Family E — Exile And Cramped Tenderness

Intent signals: `Happy Together` / `春光乍泄`, unstable couple, cramped room, travel, separation, reconciliation, emotional exhaustion.

Visible grammar:

- worn rented rooms, bars, kitchens, transit, rooftops, roadside edges, and temporary domestic spaces;
- bodies close in physical distance but divided by gaze, furniture, work, illness, or unequal depth;
- restless handheld observation alternating with exhausted stillness;
- cold exterior or daylight spill against weak warm bulbs, dirty green interiors, occasional saturated color, or a motivated monochrome passage;
- clutter and surfaces reveal a life in progress rather than serve as nostalgic decoration.

The source story is not a Hong Kong-location template. Transfer the relational blocking, provisional interiors, camera restlessness, and temperature conflict into a newly designed Hong Kong scene when Hong Kong is requested.

## Family Selection

1. Select one primary family from the user's action and emotional relationship.
2. Optionally borrow one supporting device from a second family, never its whole palette-and-prop package.
3. State the family in the recipe record, but compile only visible behavior into the generation prompt.
4. When the user gives only `王家卫感` or `港风`, default from content:
   - movement, pursuit, crowd, crossing -> Urban Temporal Flux;
   - extreme night proximity, ride, tunnel -> Nocturnal Wide-Angle Estrangement;
   - withheld two-person intimacy -> Restrained Corridor Memory;
   - solitary humid interior -> Humid Interior Drift;
   - unstable couple in a temporary room -> Exile And Cramped Tenderness.
5. Never average all families into green-red neon, grain, rain, and blur.

## Cross-Family Consistency

Keep a batch coherent through the shared core, not identical grading. Across variants, change at least two of time behavior, camera-subject relation, spatial mechanism, scene, or relational blocking. Reusing a palette while changing none of these is repetition.
