# Example — Waiting At A Street Corner

## Request

```text
一个女生凌晨站在街角等人。
```

## Route

Generate Mode. No image input. The short request is sufficiently constrained, so the Skill makes the scene, motion, light, and composition decisions without asking follow-up questions.

## Recipe

```text
[Urban Temporal Flux / street corner / pre-dawn / one woman waiting and scanning the road / Step-Printed Drift, woman held against a faster crowd clock / camera locked close to the woman while circulation crosses / Foreground Occlusion / Green Fluorescent + Amber Tungsten with a small deep-red accent / moderate-wide environmental intimacy / coarse high-speed color-film grain / restless longing]
```

## Compiled Prompt

```text
A 3:2 frame at a lived-in Hong Kong street corner just before dawn, with an aging convenience-store awning, metal shutters, a red-and-silver taxi at the far curb, and generic vertical signboards rather than a futuristic skyline. A woman in an ordinary dark jacket waits beside the curb, looking past the traffic and repeatedly checking the empty end of the street; her still posture carries restless expectation.

Lock the camera close to her at chest height and place her on the right third at medium distance. Let the blurred shoulder of a passerby enter from the near left foreground, keep her face and upper torso readable in the middle plane, and retain storefront depth, curb geometry, and a receding side street behind her. Use a moderate-wide environmental view that keeps the camera close enough for intimacy while allowing the street to press around her.

Keep her eyes, facial structure, and upper body comparatively stable as the held clock while pedestrians cross left to right at a visibly faster clock. Break their movement into uneven elongated fragments with small gaps, two or three partial coat and shoulder residues, and one body briefly occluding her lower half; attach segmented red traces only to moving taxi lights and add a faint repeated edge behind one sleeve. The architecture and road markings remain legible, with no smooth full-frame blur.

Light the corner primarily from a dirty green fluorescent tube under the awning, supported by amber tungsten from a small food stall and one restrained deep-red sign reflection on metal. Let mixed white balance contaminate one side of her skin and the shuttered storefront. Reproduce the frame with coarse high-speed color-film grain, slight halation around practicals, modest focus falloff, clipped highlights, and deep shadows that preserve her face and hands. Avoid generic cyberpunk, rainbow neon, synthetic fog, glossy advertising, beauty retouching, centered fashion posing, floating light trails, and perfect digital sharpness.
```

## Quality Focus

- The woman is the stable emotional anchor.
- Pedestrian and taxi motion have separate, plausible directions.
- The two clocks and uneven broken residues remain visible independently of color.
- The fluorescent and tungsten sources explain the palette.
- The location remains lived-in and period-plausible, not cyberpunk.
