# Example — Night Restaurant, Still Tension

## Request

```text
一家夜间街边餐馆，两个人沉默坐着，不要明显慢门，保持同一视觉家族。
```

## Route

Generate Mode with the Still Tension motion family. Overt blur would weaken the requested restraint, so suspended time is carried by blocking, narrow architecture, practical light, and only one secondary moving element.

## Recipe

```text
[Restrained Corridor Memory / night restaurant / late evening / two people sharing silence / Still Tension with a server and ceiling fan as the quiet second clock / camera locked outside the threshold / Doorway Framing / Green Fluorescent + Amber Tungsten with a small deep-red accent / normal-lens locked observation / fine grain with mild print softness / guarded intimacy]
```

## Compiled Prompt

```text
A 3:2 frame looking into a narrow, worn Hong Kong street restaurant late at night near the end of the twentieth century. Two people sit at the same small table but leave unequal space between their bodies, neither meeting the other's gaze. Stacked bowls, a metal tea urn, a handwritten generic menu, plastic stools, and a half-closed frontage make the room feel occupied and ordinary.

Observe from just outside the doorway with a normal-lens, nearly locked viewpoint. Let the doorframe and the back of an empty foreground chair partially divide the pair, keep both faces readable in the middle plane, and show a short tiled passage and working counter behind them. Their closeness in the narrow room should intensify, not resolve, their emotional distance.

Keep both diners and the architecture stable. Only a server crossing the far background and a slow ceiling fan carry subtle directional motion; use no obvious full-frame slow shutter, no repeated faces, and no floating light trails.

Light the room primarily with a tired green fluorescent tube above the counter, supported by a small amber table lamp and one restrained deep-red reflection from an exterior sign. Let mixed white balance fall unevenly across skin, tile, steel, and glass. Use fine color-film grain, mild print softness, slight halation, imperfect exposure, and deep but informative shadow. Avoid generic cyberpunk, rainbow neon, clean modern restaurant styling, centered commercial symmetry, beauty retouching, and glossy advertising light.
```

## Quality Focus

- The image stays in the family without conspicuous slow shutter.
- Architecture and unequal blocking express the relationship.
- Only secondary motion is allowed; faces and table interaction remain stable.
