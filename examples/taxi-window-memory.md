# Example — Taxi Window Memory

## Request

```text
凌晨坐在出租车后排看窗外，像在回忆刚结束的一段关系。
```

## Route

Generate Mode. The emotion is translated into reflected multi-plane space and temporal echo rather than literal symbols.

## Recipe

```text
[Urban Temporal Flux / taxi interior / after midnight / one passenger seated and watching through glass / cabin held while exterior moves as a faster clock with Vehicle Passage + Temporal Echo / camera rides with the passenger and vehicle / Window Reflection / Warm Interior + Cool Exterior with a small deep-red accent / reflected multi-plane space / pushed underexposure with soft halation / private memory]
```

## Compiled Prompt

```text
A wide horizontal frame from the back seat of a late-night Hong Kong taxi around the end of the twentieth century. One passenger sits close to the side window, shoulders turned away from the camera, watching shopfronts pass without meeting the driver's eyes. A worn vinyl seat edge, the door lock, a mechanical meter glow, and layered street reflections make the car feel used rather than staged.

Ride with the passenger from a close back-seat position and frame them off-axis through the window and a partial front-seat headrest. Keep the near cheek, eye, and hand on the window ledge readable while exterior signs, another taxi, and building columns occupy reflected planes across the glass. The close moderate-wide view compresses interior proximity against emotional distance.

Keep the passenger, seat, and window frame on the vehicle's slower clock while the exterior travels right to left at a faster clock as uneven horizontal fragments. Add one restrained repeated edge to the reflected profile and short segmented red streaks only to moving taillights. Use slight handheld drift from the moving vehicle while preserving the face; avoid transparent duplicate heads, one smooth smear, or uniform blur.

Use a dim amber cabin practical as the primary source, cool cyan-white street spill as the secondary source, and a small deep-red taxi reflection as the only accent. Let the colors overlap imperfectly on glass and skin. Reproduce the image as pushed underexposed color film with soft halation, coarse shadow grain, minor chromatic bleed, uneven focus, and selectively readable blacks. Avoid futuristic dashboards, rainbow neon, luxury-car advertising, clean orange-teal grading, studio rim light, and perfect HDR.
```

## Quality Focus

- Reflections create depth without obscuring the passenger's face.
- Exterior motion is tied to vehicle travel.
- The taxi remains an everyday period environment rather than a luxury prop.
